⚡ 3-Minute Firebase Console Setup Guide: Open Firebase Console ↗
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and click Add Project (or select your existing project).
  2. In the left sidebar, navigate to Build > Authentication > click Get Started.
  3. Under the Sign-in method tab, enable Email/Password and Google.
  4. Click the Settings gear (⚙️) > Project settings > General, scroll down to Your apps, click Web (</>), and copy the apiKey and projectId into the fields above.
  5. Click Test Firebase Setup above — your portal will instantly support Google SSO and Firebase login!
  6. Direct Firebase Management (Recommended): Under Project settings (⚙️) > Service accounts tab, click Generate new private key. Then click Install Service Account Key above and paste it (or place the file into server/serviceAccountKey.json). This gives the backend full authority to create users, save credentials, and manage passwords directly in Firebase!

Last updated: November 2026 · Conforming to Egyptian Data Protection Law & International Standards

1. Information We Collect

Aurora Group collects information that you provide voluntarily when submitting a contact inquiry or discovery brief, including your name, brand name, business email, phone number, and project requirements. We also collect minimal technical logs (IP address and browser user-agent) strictly to prevent spam and monitor service availability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is exclusively used to evaluate project feasibility, prepare commercial proposals, schedule strategic meetings, and communicate directly regarding your media and marketing campaigns. We never sell, rent, or trade client personal information.

3. Cookie & Session Management

We use secure HttpOnly session cookies to authenticate staff users securely without exposing sensitive tokens to client-side scripts. Public visitors can control performance analytics via our Cookie Preferences banner.

4. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted to our platform is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. We employ strict server-side access controls (RBAC) and row-level authorization to protect client data.

5. Your Rights & Inquiries

You have the right to request access, correction, or permanent deletion of your inquiry details. Please contact our Data Officer directly at contact.aurora.egy@gmail.com.
